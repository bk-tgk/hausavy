HAUSAVY FSBO CHECKLIST

1. Clarify your sale timeline and your minimum acceptable outcome.
2. Review nearby sold homes and active competition.
3. Choose a pricing strategy and target list price.
4. Fix obvious issues and complete cosmetic prep.
5. Declutter, clean, and stage key spaces.
6. Prepare photos, property details, and listing description.
7. Gather disclosures and process documents.
8. Decide how showings and buyer inquiries will be handled.
9. Compare offers based on terms, not just price.
10. Track escrow, contingencies, and closing handoff tasks.
