The process

A simple 10-step FSBO workflow for real homeowners

The goal is not to turn owners into agents. It is to help them move through the sale in the right order, with the right questions, without feeling lost.

01

Set your sale strategy

Define your timing, minimum acceptable price, moving plan, and whether you want to offer buyer-agent compensation.

02

Estimate value and review comps

Study nearby sold homes, current competition, condition differences, and market momentum before setting a target list price.

03

Prepare the home

Declutter, deep clean, repair obvious issues, and decide what staging or cosmetic upgrades will improve appeal.

04

Create the listing package

Write the listing description, gather measurements, features, disclosures, photos, and neighborhood highlights.

05

Launch and market

Publish the listing, share it through major channels, and make sure inquiries get a fast, professional response.

06

Schedule showings

Coordinate tours, set rules for access, and prepare a clean showing routine that works around your life.

07

Review offers

Compare price, contingencies, financing strength, closing timeline, and risk instead of looking only at the headline number.

08

Negotiate and accept

Counter with intention, document changes clearly, and know what terms matter most to protect your outcome.

09

Track escrow milestones

Monitor inspections, appraisal, contingency removals, disclosures, and requests that arise once you are in contract.

10

Close and hand off

Confirm final docs, transfer logistics, utilities, keys, and the last details that turn a contract into a completed sale.
